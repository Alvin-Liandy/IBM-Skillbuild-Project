Arctic Sea Ice Reaches Record Low in September 2023

Satellite observations confirm Arctic sea ice extent fell to 4.09 million km² — the lowest in the 45-year satellite era, raising concerns about albedo feedback loops and accelerated permafrost thaw.

Global Solar Capacity Surpasses 1 TW Milestone

The world's cumulative solar PV capacity crossed 1 terawatt for the first time in 2023, driven by record installations across Asia, Europe, and North America, cutting costs by a further 12%.

ⓘ Illustrative · Renewable Energy Agency Sep 2023

COP28 Agreement Sets New Carbon Market Framework

Nations reached consensus on Article 6 carbon market rules, enabling cross-border emissions trading and unlocking an estimated $1 trillion in climate finance flowing to developing nations by 2030.

Ocean Heat Content Hits Unprecedented Level in 2023

The global ocean absorbed more heat in 2023 than in any prior year on record, with upper ocean temperatures exceeding averages by 0.26°C — driving stronger tropical storms and threatening coral reef systems worldwide.
